# Install Setlist on your Android device

Requires **Android 8.0 or newer**. Phones and tablets are supported.
No computer, Android Studio, emulator, account or GitHub login is needed.

1. On your Android device, [download Setlist 0.0.1](https://github.com/dataandops/setlist/releases/download/v0.0.1/Setlist-0.0.1.apk).
2. Open the downloaded **Setlist-0.0.1.apk** from your browser or Downloads.
3. If Android asks for permission, tap **Settings**, enable **Allow from this source** for the browser or Files app you used, then return to the installer.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

This build is distributed directly, rather than through Google Play. Android's
permission wording varies by device; [Google's installation help](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7391672) explains the source setting.
If the device blocks installation with a different message, send the exact message
back to the person who shared the app.

## Try it

- Tap **Try a sample set**, open it, then **Start setlist**.
- Use **Next page** to turn pages. At the end of a song it becomes **Next song**.
- Tap **Setlist** to jump to another song or return to editing.

## Add your music

1. Save your PDFs on the Android device, such as in **Downloads**.
2. Tap **New setlist**, enter its name, then **Import PDFs**.
3. Long-press one PDF, select any others, then tap **Open** or **Select**.
4. Hold a song's **≡** handle to drag it into playing order.
5. Tap a song for options: rename, attach an MP3, or remove it from the set.

Your PDFs and MP3s stay in their original locations. Setlist remembers permission
to read them and does not make permanent copies. If you move a PDF, use the song's
**Locate / replace PDF** option. Audio plays only after you press **Play MP3**.

For future updates, install the new APK over this one to keep your saved setlists.
Uninstalling the app removes its saved setlists and file links; original music
files remain on your device.

Swipe left on the score to advance a page; swipe right to go back. At a song boundary, swiping continues to the adjacent song. The last page opens the set-complete dialog. Taps, short movements and vertical drags do not turn pages. Buttons remain available.
